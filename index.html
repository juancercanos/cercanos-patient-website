Getting Started

Simple steps to better health

Getting started with your personal care management app takes just a few minutes.

1

Download the App

Get the Itera Health app from the App Store or Google Play. It's free for all Cercanos patients.

2

Activate Your Account

You will receive an email from your Cercanos care team with instructions to activate your account and set up your personal health profile.

3

Connect Your Devices

Pair your blood pressure monitor, glucometer, or other health devices for automatic tracking.

4

Start Your Journey

Follow your personalized care plan, track your progress, and connect with your care team anytime.

App Features

Everything you need to manage your care

Powered by Itera Health's connected care ecosystem, designed with the warmth and cultural understanding of Cercanos.

Real-Time Health Monitoring

Track your vitals, blood pressure, glucose levels, and more. Connected medical-grade devices sync automatically with your care plan.

Care Team Connection

Stay connected with your doctors, nutritionists, health coaches, and care coordinators. Secure messaging keeps your whole team in sync.

Personalized Care Plans

Dynamic care plans that adapt to your progress. Your treatment pathway evolves based on real-time data from your daily health activities.

Smart Reminders

Never miss a medication, appointment, or health check. Personalized alerts keep you on track with your wellness goals every day.

Health Education

Access bilingual educational content about your conditions. Culturally relevant nutrition guides, exercise tips, and wellness resources at your fingertips.

Secure Messaging

Communicate directly with your care team in your preferred language. Share updates, ask questions, and receive guidance when you need it most.

Patient Benefits

Why patients love using the app

Designed with you in mind, culturally sensitive and available in English and Spanish.

24/7 Access to Your Care Team

Reach your health coach, nutritionist, or care coordinator whenever you need support. No more waiting for office hours.

Culturally Competent Care

Content and communication designed for Hispanic communities. Bilingual support with culturally relevant health guidance.

Track Your Progress

See how far you've come with visual dashboards. Celebrate milestones and stay motivated on your path to better health.

Secure and Private

Your health data is encrypted and protected. HIPAA-compliant platform ensures your information stays safe and confidential.

Complete Health Records

Access your care plans, lab results, medication lists, and visit summaries all in one place. Share them easily with any provider.

Family Caregiver Access

Invite family members to view your care plan and progress. Keep your loved ones informed and involved in your health journey.
